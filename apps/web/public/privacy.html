Privacy Policy

Emulsion · last updated 13 November 2026

Emulsion collects no data. The app does not collect personal data, usage data, or device identifiers.

This covers the Emulsion website, its installable web app, and the Emulsion app for iPhone.

No accounts, no analytics, no uploads

There are no user accounts. There is no advertising and no third-party tracking. Every image is generated on your own device, by your own hardware, and never leaves it unless you deliberately share it yourself.

Saved sessions and offline use

The web app stores your current settings and undo history in this browser so you can return to your wallpaper. A service worker caches the app and its font for offline use. Neither is sent to us. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes both. The iPhone app saves sessions locally too.

Photos

If you save an image, the app asks permission to add photos to your library. This is add-only access: it can write the image you asked it to save, and it cannot read, browse, or see anything already in your library.

In the web app, Save downloads a file and Share opens your device’s share sheet when supported. The web app does not request access to your photo library.

Hosting

The website is delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, it receives connection information such as your IP address when you load the site. Emulsion does not add analytics or tracking scripts.

What you make is yours

Wallpapers you export belong to you, with no restriction on how you use them. They are written where you tell the system to put them and are never copied anywhere else.

Children

Emulsion is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date above changes with it. The repository keeps the full history of every revision.
